# Anuradha Weeraman's Blog

Welcome to my personal blog. This space is a collection of my thoughts, explorations, and deep dives into the world of technology, cultivated over a career in software engineering. It's a journal of my curiosities, from the intricate workings of a Linux kernel to the philosophical underpinnings of the open-source movement.

## What You'll Find Here

My writing spans a wide range of topics over many years, often focusing on the foundational principles and technologies that power our digital world. Some of the core themes include:

*   **Systems Programming & Kernel Development:** Exploring the depths of operating systems, a passion of mine since childhood.
*   **The World of Open Source:** Reflections on the free software movement, philosophy, community, and key figures.
*   **Distributed Systems & Cloud Native:** Journeys into modern infrastructure.
*   **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:** Demystifying complex topics like AlphaGo's architecture and exploring the frontiers of AI.
*   **Security & Cryptography:** Understanding key aspects of digital security.
*   **Hardware & Embedded Systems:** Notes from my escapades in tinkering with hardware.
*   **Personal Reflections & Stories:** Sharing personal and life experiences and learnings.

In a way, this blog also chronicles my journey in technology and is a personal record that I keep to track my own progression in life.

Thank you for visiting. I hope you find something that sparks your curiosity.
